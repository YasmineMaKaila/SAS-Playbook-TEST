## How to use this report

For each row, an SME or Talent reviewer should do one of the following:
1. **Confirm the source is correct as written** — no change needed; flag can be closed.
2. **Provide corrected/clarified wording** — the derived pod data will be updated from the approved Word source (or a documented correction to it), not edited ad hoc in the interface.
3. **Confirm the ambiguity is intentional or acceptable** — e.g., regional policy genuinely differs pod-to-pod.

No item in this report has been auto-resolved. `humanVerifiedDate` and `verifiedBy` remain unset for every pod until an authorized reviewer signs off — a source refresh is never treated as equivalent to operational verification.
