## Methodology

1. **Source extraction:** SAS Playbook Info 2026.docx was read in full for each of the nine pods completed in this build (GA 1, NC 3, North FL 4, South FL 6, OH/TN 7, VA 8, North Dallas 9, HASA 11, AZ 13). Pods 10 (CFW) and 12 (Mountain West) were carried forward from the approved prototype, unchanged.
2. **Classification:** Every source statement was assigned to General Pod Information (applies pod-wide) or Property-Specific Information (unique to one named community), per the rule: pod-wide → general; unusual and tied to one named property → property-specific. Judgment calls (e.g. grouping a shared program across several named communities, or treating a two-tier fee schedule as the pod's actual general rate structure rather than as exceptions) are called out in the per-pod detail above and, where non-obvious, in the review-flag report.
3. **Verbatim preservation:** Operational wording, amounts, and dates were preserved exactly as written in the source. The only characters removed were Word's bold/italic markdown-style asterisks, which are formatting artifacts of the text extraction rather than literal characters in the source document; no words were changed. A small number of run-together words left by that same extraction artifact (e.g. "Move****ins") were restored to their correct hyphenated form ("Move-ins") by cross-referencing the identical, unambiguous phrase used elsewhere in the same document without formatting artifacts.
4. **Fact-presence verification:** An automated check confirmed every distinctive fact transcribed from the source (every dollar figure, named property, phase/building reference, and key phrase identified during classification) is present somewhere in the final rendered data for its pod. Two apparent gaps surfaced during this check and were confirmed to be checker limitations (property names that live in a button label rather than in list text), not actual content gaps.
5. **No duplication:** Property-specific content is never also rendered in General Pod Information — verified structurally (property-exception sections are either excluded from the general render pass or were never added to it) and confirmed by direct inspection of rendered output for every pod.
