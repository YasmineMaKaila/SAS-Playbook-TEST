## Methodology

1. Extracted every named community from all 11 pods' approved data, including names that only appear inside general pod-wide lists (fraud watch, community-type categorizations, pet-rent tiers, voucher lists, etc.) — not just names that already have their own property dropdown.
2. Cross-referenced each name against the `Community`, `Combined Community Name`, and `Former Name(s)` columns of the approved portfolio listing, using each pod's known state/region to discard cross-region false matches (e.g. a generic word match landing on an unrelated property in another state).
3. Where the portfolio's own data was itself ambiguous (two different entries plausibly matching one source name) or entirely absent, the result is flagged rather than resolved by best guess, per your explicit instruction.
4. The only two names actually written into the live interface are the ones you explicitly confirmed in your message (Cortland Duluth, Cortland at the Village) — both of which the portfolio also independently corroborates.
